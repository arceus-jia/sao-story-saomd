　2) Verify your data transfer ID and

　must be between 8 and 32 alphanumeric  
　characters long.)  
　3) Once you have set your password, the  
　data transfer settings will conclude.  
　CAUTION:  
　- Please keep your password private.  
　- Please make a note of your data  
　transfer ID.  
　- Your password can be changed later.  
　- When transferring data to a different  
　Store, Memory Diamonds will be lost. (This  
　includes all Memory Diamonds  
　distributed for free.)\</body\_text\>

　it triggers the effect Crossing Link which boosts the attributes of both characters.  
　  
　Crossing Link has a variety of effects.   
　It can boost character parameters, share MP charge amount   
　from regular attacks with Stand-by character,   
　as well as other effects.  
　  
　■Additional Attack  
　Activating Crossing Skill can trigger an additional wide-ranged attack centered   
　on the enemy that emits a large shock wave effect.  
　  
　Additional Attacks are only activated with a Partner.  
　  
　\[Attention\]  
　■When SS3 is activated on a Crossing Skill character, attack speed is set. Status boost effects and changes in weapon type attack speed do not apply.  
　  
　■ "Crossing Link" and "Additional Attack" will not activate if a character is staggered   
　or has their skill interrupted while activating Crossing Skill.  
　  
　■Crossing Link boosted attributes may result in duplicate effects depending on one's Sword Skill.  
　  
　■Only one type of effect can be activated at a time with Crossing Link.  
　If another Crossing Link is activated while the effect of a previous one is still active,   
　the new effect will activate over the current one.\</body\_text\>
